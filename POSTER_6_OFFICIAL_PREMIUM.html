ABSTRACT
With the rapid advancement in AI and computer vision, automated vehicle damage detection has emerged as a significant area of research and application. Manual assessment is prone to human error, fraud, and is time-consuming — affecting insurance, vehicle rental, and resale. CARPER — The Car Problem Solver is a smart web-based application that detects exterior vehicle damage and provides an estimated repair cost. It uses YOLOv8n trained on the CarDD dataset (4,000 images, 6 classes). For cost estimation, Linear Regression, Random Forest, Extra Trees and Gradient Boosting were evaluated; Gradient Boosting was selected on best performance. The platform follows a Client–Server three-tier architecture (Next.js · NestJS · PostgreSQL), with semantic search and price-matching algorithms connecting users to affordable replacement parts.

CONCLUSION
CARPER successfully demonstrates that a lightweight YOLOv8n trained on the CarDD dataset can deliver competitive multi-class vehicle damage detection at real-time inference speed (4.4 ms/image), achieving an overall mAP@0.5 of 0.732 with particularly strong performance on Glass Shatter (0.979), Tire Flat (0.934) and Lamp Broken (0.871). The integrated cost-estimation module and semantic part-recommendation pipeline extend CARPER into a practical, end-to-end automotive assessment tool. The system fulfils all three project success criteria and is presented as a fully containerised, web-deployed solution suitable for insurance automation, vehicle rental services, and private resale valuation.
