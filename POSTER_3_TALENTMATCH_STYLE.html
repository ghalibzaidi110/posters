AABSTRACT
CARPER — The Car Problem Solver is an intelligent web platform that unifies computer vision, machine-learning regression, and natural-language semantic search to streamline vehicle damage assessment. The platform automates exterior-damage detection, repair-cost estimation, and affordable replacement-part recommendation. Built with Next.js 15, NestJS 11, PostgreSQL, YOLOv8n, Gradient Boosting and Sentence-BERT, it delivers transparent assessments, accelerates insurance & rental workflows, and lowers the cost of vehicle inspection for owners, businesses, and verifiers.

01

CCONCLUSION
CARPER demonstrates that a lightweight YOLOv8n trained on CarDD can deliver competitive multi-class damage detection at real-time speeds — 0.732 mAP@0.5 overall and standout results on Glass Shatter (0.979), Tire Flat (0.934) & Lamp Broken (0.871). Coupled with a Gradient-Boosting cost estimator and Sentence-BERT-driven part recommendation, CARPER is a complete, containerised, web-deployed automotive-assessment tool ready for insurance, rental, and resale workflows.
