ABSTRACT
Manual car damage assessment is prone to human error, fraud, and is time-consuming — affecting insurance, vehicle rental, and resale. CARPER — The Car Problem Solver is a smart web-based application that detects exterior vehicle damage and provides an estimated repair cost. It uses the YOLOv8n object-detection model trained on the CarDD dataset (4,000 high-resolution images, 6 damage classes: dent, scratch, crack, glass shatter, tire flat, lamp broken). For cost estimation, Linear Regression, Random Forest, Extra Trees and Gradient Boosting were evaluated; Gradient Boosting was selected. The platform follows a Client–Server three-tier architecture: Next.js · NestJS · PostgreSQL. Semantic search and price-matching algorithms connect users to affordable replacement parts from online marketplaces.

OBJECTIVE
The project delivers a web-based automated vehicle damage inspection by:
  • Detecting six external damage types using YOLOv8n on CarDD.
  • Estimating repair cost via Gradient Boosting regression.
  • Recommending affordable parts via semantic marketplace search.
  • Providing a seamless web interface for owners, rentals & admins.

CONCLUSION
CARPER successfully demonstrates that a lightweight YOLOv8n model trained on the CarDD dataset can deliver competitive multi-class vehicle damage detection at real-time inference speed (4.4 ms/image), achieving an overall mAP@0.5 of 0.732 with particularly strong performance on Glass Shatter (0.979), Tire Flat (0.934), and Lamp Broken (0.871). The integrated cost estimation module and semantic part-recommendation pipeline further extend CARPER into a practical, end-to-end automotive assessment tool. The system fulfils all three project success criteria and is presented as a fully containerised, web-deployed solution suitable for insurance automation, vehicle rental services, and private resale valuation.
