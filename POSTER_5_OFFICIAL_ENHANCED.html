ABSTRACT
With the rapid advancement in AI and computer vision, automated vehicle damage detection has emerged as a significant area of research and application. Manual assessment is prone to human error, fraud, and is time-consuming — affecting insurance, vehicle rental and resale. CARPER — The Car Problem Solver is a smart web-based application that detects exterior vehicle damage and provides an estimated repair cost. It uses YOLOv8n trained on CarDD (4,000 high-resolution images, 6 damage classes). For cost estimation, Linear Regression, Random Forest, Extra Trees and Gradient Boosting were evaluated; Gradient Boosting was selected. The platform follows a Client–Server three-tier architecture: Next.js · NestJS · PostgreSQL. Semantic search and price-matching connect users to affordable replacement parts.
OBJECTIVE
  • Detect 6 damage classes using YOLOv8n on CarDD.
  • Estimate repair cost via Gradient Boosting.
  • Recommend affordable parts via semantic search.
  • Deliver a unified web interface for owners, rentals, admin.

CONCLUSION
CARPER demonstrates that a lightweight YOLOv8n trained on the CarDD dataset can deliver competitive multi-class vehicle damage detection at real-time inference speed (4.4 ms/image), with an overall mAP@0.5 of 0.732 and standout per-class performance on Glass Shatter (0.979), Tire Flat (0.934) and Lamp Broken (0.871). The integrated cost-estimation module and semantic part-recommendation pipeline extend CARPER into a practical, end-to-end automotive assessment tool. The system fulfils all three success criteria and is deployed as a fully containerised web solution suitable for insurance, rental, and resale.
