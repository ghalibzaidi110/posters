PROBLEM STATEMENT
Manual exterior-damage assessment in insurance, vehicle rental, and resale is slow, prone to human error, and easily exploited by fraud. Inconsistent estimates inflate claims, dispute settlements, and erode buyer trust. CARPER replaces subjective inspection with a computer-vision pipeline that detects damage, estimates repair cost, and recommends affordable parts — through a single web app.
KEY ISSUES SOLVED
  • Time-consuming manual inspection
  • Subjective, inconsistent damage rating
  • Inflated / fraudulent repair quotes
  • No transparent price benchmarking for parts
  • Fragmented rental & resale workflows
OBJECTIVES

① DETECT

Detect 6 external damage classes — Dent, Scratch, Crack, Glass Shatter, Tire Flat, Lamp Broken — using YOLOv8n trained on the CarDD dataset (4,000 images).

② ESTIMATE

Predict repair cost via Gradient Boosting (benchmarked vs. Linear Regression, Random Forest, Extra Trees) using detected severity, part, and vehicle features.

③ RECOMMEND

Surface affordable replacement parts through semantic search & price-matching across online marketplaces, plus a unified web interface for owners, renters & admins.

CONCLUSION
CARPER demonstrates that a lightweight YOLOv8n trained on the CarDD dataset can deliver competitive multi-class vehicle damage detection at real-time inference speed (4.4 ms/image) with overall mAP@0.5 = 0.732 — and remarkable per-class performance on Glass Shatter (0.979), Tire Flat (0.934) and Lamp Broken (0.871). The integrated cost estimation module and semantic part-recommendation pipeline extend CARPER into a practical, end-to-end automotive assessment tool. The system fulfils all three project success criteria and is presented as a fully containerised, web-deployed solution suitable for insurance automation, vehicle-rental services, and private resale valuation.
